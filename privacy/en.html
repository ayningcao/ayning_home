HairZen Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 13, 2026

HairZen is a personal AI hairstyle assistant. We designed it around one rule: anything that can be processed on your device never leaves it. This notice explains what stays on your device, what is uploaded only when you ask for it, how it is handled, and the rights you have.

1. Work that happens only on your device

  • Face detection and photo analysis: face-shape analysis for camera try-on and hair scans runs on-device via Apple's Vision framework. No photo is uploaded during this step.
  • Render history: your generated results are stored locally on the device, isolated per account, never automatically uploaded or synced.

2. What happens only when you ask

Only when you actively tap "Generate" (AI try-on, 3D model, spin video) is the selected photo uploaded to our servers to produce your result:

  • The photo is used solely to render the effect you requested — never for advertising, never for training;
  • Our servers do not keep originals or results long-term: temporary data is deleted shortly after rendering completes;
  • During rendering the photo is shared only with AI generation providers we have engaged (image, 3D and video), whose processing is limited to producing your requested result.

3. Other information we collect

  • Account identifiers: after sign-in we store only the minimum necessary identifier (e.g. your Apple sign-in identifier). We do not collect names or phone numbers.
  • Device and network information: to prevent free-trial abuse we use device credentials and IP addresses for rate limiting and duplicate-claim detection; identifiers in logs are masked.
  • Credits and orders: your credit balance, usage and purchase records (for reconciliation and refunds).

4. Third parties

  • Apple: in-app purchases, sign-in and DeviceCheck.
  • Engaged AI generation providers: receive photos only when you actively render.
  • WeChat: WeChat sign-in and WeChat in-app payment are used only in the WeChat Mini Program version; the iOS app does not use them.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Deleting the app or clearing history removes all local data on the device;
  • After account deletion, server-side account data is deleted or de-identified;
  • Uploaded photos and generated results are processed temporarily and are not retained as long-term data.

6. Children

The app is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information without guardian consent, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

We do not sell your personal information. Under GDPR, CCPA and other applicable laws you may exercise rights of access, rectification, deletion, restriction and withdrawal of consent, including account deletion to remove server-side data. Email ayningai@outlook.com to exercise these rights or lodge a complaint.

8. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Logs never contain full face images, full device identifiers or raw IP addresses. Access keys are stored only on our servers.

9. Changes to this notice

If our processing changes materially, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated notice.
