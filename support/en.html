FAQ

What are credits and how do I get them?

Generating an AI effect consumes credits. New users receive free credits; you can also buy credit packs in the app or earn credits by inviting friends.

Am I charged if a generation fails?

No. If rendering fails, the credits are automatically returned to your account — no request needed.

How long does generation take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Videos and 3D models may take a bit longer. You can leave the page while it runs and check the record later.

How do I save my result?

Tap "Save to Photos" when the result is ready. History is stored on your device, isolated per account.

Will my photos be made public?

No. Face detection runs on your device; photos are uploaded only when you actively generate an effect, and are used solely to produce that result — never for advertising or model training.

Not happy with a result?

Try a different hairstyle or color and generate again. A well-lit, front-facing photo gives the most realistic output.

How do I request a refund?

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Go to Settings → Apple Account → Media & Purchases → Report a Problem, or contact Apple Support.

When writing to us, please include

  • Device model and iOS version;
  • App version (Profile → About);
  • Approximate time of the issue and a screenshot, so we can locate it faster.

System requirements

Please use a recent iOS version and keep the app up to date. Some features (camera, saving to Photos) require the corresponding permissions.
