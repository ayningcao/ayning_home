隐私说明

我们不会把你的图片上传到服务器或保存在服务器。人脸检测与脸型分析全部在你的设备本地完成，不需要联网。

一、图片与人脸数据

  • 实时试戴、AI 发型检测和脸型分析均使用设备本地能力（Apple Vision / 端侧模型），照片不会因为检测或分析而上传。
  • 我们不会将照片写入服务器数据库、磁盘或对象存储，也不会将照片用于模型训练、广告或出售。
  • 如果你把渲染结果保存在 App 内，渲染历史仅保存在你的设备本地，你可以随时在 App 内删除。

二、什么时候会发送照片

只有当你主动点击生成云端真实效果或 3D 效果时，所选照片和发型参数才会通过 HTTPS 加密连接发送给第三方大模型服务商，仅用于完成本次渲染。你未主动触发时，不会发送。

照片只在本次渲染任务期间短暂驻留内存，渲染结果在你取走前短暂缓存；我们不会长期保存照片或渲染结果。

三、我们收集的必要信息

为了登录、计费、防刷和售后，服务端可能保存设备标识、账号信息、积分与订单记录、渲染任务状态等必要数据；这些数据不包含你的照片。我们不会收集你的位置、通讯录或相册元数据。

四、第三方模型服务与免责声明

云端 AI 渲染由第三方大模型服务商提供（如火山引擎·豆包），云计算基础设施可能由阿里云提供。服务商仅接收完成本次渲染所必需的照片与参数；其处理行为受其隐私政策约束。

服务商承诺不会利用你的人脸数据进行模型训练。如服务商违反该承诺或违规使用数据，由该服务商依法承担相应责任。AI 生成结果仅供试戴与造型参考，不构成美发、医疗或其他专业建议。

五、保留、删除与你的权利

你可以随时删除本地渲染历史，也可以申请注销账号。注销后，登录身份、会话和未使用积分按规则处理；支付、退款、渲染任务等记录可能依法律要求保留。

六、联系我们

隐私问题、投诉或数据删除请求请联系：ayningai@outlook.com。

Privacy Notice

We do not upload your photos to our servers or store them on our servers. Face detection and face-shape analysis run entirely on your device and do not require an internet connection.

1. Photos and facial data

  • Live try-on, AI hair scan, and face-shape analysis use on-device capabilities (Apple Vision / on-device models). Photos are not uploaded for detection or analysis.
  • We do not write photos to server databases, disks, or object storage, and do not use photos for model training, advertising, or sale.
  • If you keep a render in the app, the render history stays only on your device and can be deleted in the app at any time.

2. When a photo is sent

Only when you actively generate a cloud-rendered realistic or 3D result will the selected photo and hairstyle parameters be sent over encrypted HTTPS to a third-party large-model provider, solely to complete that render. Nothing is sent unless you trigger it.

Photos remain briefly in memory only for the render task, and results are briefly cached until you retrieve them. We do not retain photos or render results long-term.

3. Necessary information we collect

For login, billing, abuse prevention, and support, our servers may store a device identifier, account information, credit and order records, and render task status. These records do not contain your photos. We do not collect your location, contacts, or photo-library metadata.

4. Third-party model services and disclaimer

Cloud AI rendering is provided by third-party large-model providers (such as Volcano Engine Doubao), and cloud infrastructure may be provided by Alibaba Cloud. Providers receive only the photo and parameters necessary for the requested render, and their processing is governed by their privacy policies.

The providers represent that they will not use your facial data for model training. If a provider breaches this commitment or misuses the data, that provider bears the corresponding legal responsibility. AI-generated results are for try-on and styling reference only and do not constitute hairdressing, medical, or other professional advice.

5. Retention, deletion, and your rights

You can delete local render history at any time and request account deletion. After deletion, login identities, sessions, and unused credits are handled according to the applicable rules; payment, refund, and render-task records may be retained as required by law.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, complaints, or deletion requests, contact ayningai@outlook.com.
