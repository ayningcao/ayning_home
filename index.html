A hairstyle assistant, not a filter.

Every render is built for one decision: whether to actually make the change.

Real results, not stickers

A generative model repaints your own photo instead of pasting clip-art hair. Lighting, skin tone and hair texture stay consistent with the shot.

Know your face shape first

An on-device scan measures your proportions — jaw, cheekbones, hairline — then recommends styles that actually suit them.

Browse before you book

Curated libraries for women, men and playful looks, each with the reason it works — so the salon conversation starts from a clear idea.

See it in motion

Turn a render into a short spin video to check how the length and color read from every angle — not just the front.

Three taps from photo to preview.

Pick a style, keep the photo, and the result usually lands in a minute or two — it keeps running even if you leave the page.

Your face stays yours.

Face detection and shape analysis run on your iPhone. A photo is uploaded only when you ask for a render, used only for that render, and not kept on our servers long-term.
